FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is KwikWork free for job seekers?
Yes — always. Browsing and applying costs nothing and no account is required. Employers cover all placement costs.
How do I apply for a role?
Tap “Apply by email” on any listing. Your email app opens pre-addressed to our recruitment team — attach your resume and send. We forward complete applications to the employer the same day.
How quickly can employers see candidates?
Typically within 48 hours of receiving your job brief. For temporary cover, we can often provide same-day or next-day candidates.
What does it cost employers?
Listing a role is free. A placement fee applies only when you hire — contact us for a quote tailored to the role. Every placement includes a replacement guarantee.
How are candidates vetted?
Every candidate is ID-verified, reference-checked, and screened for core office skills and software proficiency before joining a shortlist.
Which locations do you serve?
All 50 states — on-site placements in major metros plus fully remote roles across the country.
