## Posting tips

1. **Start with Ad 1 (general)**, then post the role-specific ads in
   different categories or groups so they don't compete with each other.
2. On craigslist, post **one ad per city per role** — duplicate posts in the
   same city can get flagged.
3. Rotate your posts every few days and refresh the wording slightly.
4. ALL applications go through the Fillout form
   (https://forms.fillout.com/t/kLSRLen5kjus) — check responses in your
   Fillout dashboard. The support@ email forwarder still catches employer
   inquiries.
5. Call/text line for questions: **+1 (832) 268-8854**
