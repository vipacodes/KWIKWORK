Straight answers

SEC. 05 — FAQ
Is this allowed by the platforms?
Guidance, coaching and preparation are allowed. What platforms prohibit — and what we never do — is sharing accounts, working under someone else's identity, or completing assessments for another person. You apply, sit assessments and complete every task yourself. We make sure you do those things well.
What does it cost?
Nothing upfront. We take a percentage of what you earn on the platforms while under our management, settled monthly on money you've actually received. The exact rate is agreed in writing before we begin.
Do you guarantee I'll be accepted or pass?
No — and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. Acceptance depends on each platform's demand, your region and your skills. What we control is preparation: avoiding the mistakes that sink applications, and getting your assessment technique to the standard reviewers reward. That meaningfully improves your odds; it doesn't guarantee them.
Which countries do you work with?
We take applicants worldwide. Opportunities differ significantly by country and language — some platforms are restricted by region, others pay more for particular languages or time zones. The intro call is where we tell you honestly what's realistic where you are.
Do I need experience?
For general tasks, strong written English and careful attention to detail are often enough. For specialist tracks — law, medicine, coding, finance, advanced maths — platforms pay more but want verifiable expertise. Complete beginners get onboarded with us regularly; it just takes a little longer.
How long does onboarding take?
Anywhere from a few days to several weeks, depending on the platform and demand — queue times genuinely fluctuate. We manage that waiting time productively: applying across multiple platforms and preparing you meanwhile, so you're not sitting on one application.
What do I need to take part?
A reliable internet connection, a laptop or desktop computer (most platforms don't allow phone work), a quiet workspace, and valid identification. That's the honest minimum.
Someone offering "guaranteed passes" contacted me. Should I take it?
No. That offer — someone sitting the assessment for you, or selling leaked answers — is the most reliable way to lose your account, forfeit unpaid earnings, and be permanently banned across platforms. It's also the reason a service like ours insists on doing things the honest way.
